**Armor of the Ages** is a Minecraft mod that adds animated armor sets inspired by cultures from across history. Craft the armor of an Egyptian pharaoh, a Roman centurion or a Japanese samurai, and unlock a unique set bonus when you wear the full set.

Part of the **[Dawn of Time](https://www.curseforge.com/members/dawnoftime_team/projects)** mod ecosystem.

<div align="center"><img src="https://i.imgur.com/yhha6Zo.png"></div>

## Features

- **10 armor sets and a hat**: every piece has a custom animated 3D model.
- **Set bonuses**: wear the 4 pieces of a set to gain special effects, some of them depending on the biome, the weather, the time of day, your health or the dimension you are in.
- **Male and female models**: the armor adapts to your skin type (classic or slim), and you can switch it yourself in the config.
- **Armor skins**: change the look of your armor from the config, and choose whether other players can see it.
- **Natural loot**: armor pieces can be found in the chests of vanilla structures.
- **Fully configurable**: durability, defense, enchantability and toughness of every set can be changed in game.

## Armor Sets

Every set is crafted from the matching vanilla armor piece surrounded by its own materials. All recipes are visible in the recipe book.

| Set | Crafted from | Set bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron Plate** | Iron chestplate and iron blocks | **Man of Steel**: Resistance I |
| **Dō-maru** | Leather armor, leather and string | **Night Butterfly**: Speed I at night, Strength I while sneaking |
| **O-Yoroi** | Iron armor, coal blocks and redstone blocks | **Ancestral Strength**: Strength I |
| **Raijin** | Netherite armor and gold | **Calm as Thunder**: stronger as the weather gets worse |
| **Centurion** | Iron armor, gold and red dye | **Spartan Stamina**: Saturation I |
| **Pharaoh** | Diamond armor, gold blocks and string | **Son of the Dunes**: Fire Resistance, Glowing, Haste I in the desert |
| **Anubis** | Netherite armor, gold, lapis and coal blocks | **Father of the Undead**: Health Boost and Resistance, at the cost of Wither |
| **Quetzalcoatl** | Netherite armor, gold, feathers and bone blocks | **Venom Blood**: Strength grows as your health drops, at the cost of Hunger and Poison |
| **Holy** | Netherite armor, iron and gold blocks | **Protector of the Weak**: Resistance I, upgraded with Regeneration when monsters are near |
| **Exalted Aurum** | Netherite armor, gold blocks and red wool | **Creature of the Abyss**: effects change with depth and dimension |
| **Bamboo Hat** | Bamboo | **Spirit of the Field**: Haste I when worn with the O-Yoroi chestplate, leggings and boots |

## Set Bonuses in Detail

Set bonuses only apply when the full set is worn. Take off one piece and the effects stop immediately.

| Set | Effects |
|---|---|
| **Raijin** | Clear sky: Speed I, Jump Boost II and Slow Falling. Rain: Speed II and Strength I. Thunderstorm: Speed II and Strength II |
| **Exalted Aurum** | Surface: Mining Fatigue and Weakness. Caves: Weakness and Resistance I. Deep underground: Fire Resistance and Resistance I. Nether: Fire Resistance II and Resistance I. End: Fire Resistance II and Resistance II |
| **Quetzalcoatl** | Always Hunger and Poison. Strength I above 50% health, Strength II below 50%, Strength III below 20% |
| **Anubis** | Always Health Boost and Wither. Resistance I above 50% health, Resistance II below 50%, plus Strength I below 20% |
| **Holy** | Resistance I at all times. Resistance II and Regeneration when a monster is within 16 blocks |
| **Pharaoh** | Fire Resistance and Glowing at all times. Haste I in the desert |
| **Dō-maru** | Speed I during the night. Strength I while sneaking |

## Armor Skins

Change the theme of your armor from the **Armor Skins** tab of the config. Each skin applies to the whole set, and you can choose to share it with other players or keep it for yourself.

| Set | Available skins |
|---|---|
| **O-Yoroi** | Default, Nightblue, Amber, Jade, Pinky, Dusk, Ink |
| **Iron Plate** | Default, Ashes |

**Patreon skins**: supporters of the Dawn of Time team unlock exclusive skins depending on their tier: Silver Centurion, Silver Pharaoh, Silver Raijin, Crystal Raijin and Amethyst O-Yoroi. More are on the way. Skins are matched to your Minecraft account automatically.

## Configuration

Armor of the Ages uses **[YACL (Yet Another Config Lib)](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl)** for its in game configuration screen.

| Tab | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Body Type** | Male or female armor model, synced between players on a server |
| **Armor Properties** | Durability, defense per slot, enchantability, toughness and knockback resistance of each set. Changes apply on the next game launch |
| **Armor Loot** | Enable or disable chest loot globally or for each set |
| **Armor Skins** | Pick a skin for each set and choose whether to share it with other players |
| **Patreon Skins** | Preview exclusive skins and see which tier unlocks them |

Settings are saved in `config/armoroftheages.json`. On Fabric, the config screen is reachable through [ModMenu](https://modrinth.com/mod/modmenu).

## Overriding Recipes

All recipes can be overridden or removed with a datapack, without modifying the mod. This is useful for modpacks and servers.

**Datapack structure:**

```
my_datapack/
├── pack.mcmeta
└── data/
    └── armoroftheages/
        └── recipe/
            └── <recipe_name>.json
```

**`pack.mcmeta`** (at the root of the datapack):

```json
{
    "pack": {
        "pack_format": 48,
        "description": "My custom AotA recipes"
    }
}
```

`pack_format: 48` is the correct value for Minecraft 1.21.1.

**Installation:** place the datapack folder (or its `.zip`) in `.minecraft/saves/<world_name>/datapacks/` for single player, or in `<server_root>/world/datapacks/` for a server. Then run `/reload` in game or restart the server.

**Override a recipe** by adding a `.json` file with the same name as the original. The full list of recipes is on GitHub: [recipes](https://github.com/DawnOfTimeMC/armoroftheages/tree/1.21.1/common/src/main/resources/data/armoroftheages/recipe).

**Remove a recipe** by overriding its file with an empty JSON object:

```json
{}
```

Minecraft logs a warning and skips the recipe. The game loads normally, this is not a crash.
